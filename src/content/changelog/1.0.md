### Your map

- **My World** fills in every country and US state you've been to, with each spot you've saved as a pin. Tap a pin to see what you thought of it.
- **Tick countries** you went to before you started, so your count is right from day one.
- **Choose how your map looks**: its colours, the shape of your pins and the colour of your home.

### Trips and spots

- **Add a trip** in a minute, or add years of past trips at once with Quick add. A rough date is fine.
- **Spots** are the places you went: search for them, give them stars, mark your favourites and note what not to miss.
- Long lists sort themselves by kind, with your favourites first.
- **A cover photo** for each trip, alongside its map.

### Planning what's next

- **Plan a trip coming up** day by day, with a list of ideas for anything without a day yet.
- **Where to Next** keeps your trips coming up and your wishlist of places you want to go, with a countdown to the next one.

### Destinations and home

- **Destinations** gathers every city and country you've been to, with all your spots there across every trip, and a note of your own for each.
- **Home**: add where you live, and keep spots near home. Past homes stay on your map.

### Trip costs

- **Add expenses** in any currency, split however you like among the people who went.
- Totals are in your own currency, and **Settle up** shows who pays whom.

### Sharing

- **Two kinds of link** for every trip: one with your tips for friends, and one with the costs for the people who came, who can join the trip.
- Friends can **save your spots** to their own wishlist straight from your link.

### Yours to keep

- **Your stats**: countries, cities, days away and what it all cost.
- **Download your data** at any time, and restore it from a backup.
- **Bring in your saved places from Google Maps.**
- Works on your phone and your computer, in light or dark.
